Smart Vision Writer

How it works

A PIR sensor detects a person and triggers the ESP32-CAM to capture a JPEG. The image goes over local Wi-Fi to a Flask server, where BLIP writes a caption and CLIP scores it against custom labels. A Streamlit dashboard shows the result.

PIR → ESP32-CAM → HTTP → Flask → BLIP + CLIP → Streamlit

Features

  • PIR-triggered capture from an HC-SR501 sensor
  • Zero-shot classification with no retraining
  • Confidence scores, progress bars and a gallery of the last 12 captures
  • Upload mode to test any image without the hardware
  • Dual-core firmware: PIR on core 0, camera server on core 1
  • Privacy-first: all data stays on the local network

Engineering decision

The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor was replaced with a PIR sensor because its 5V ECHO pin exceeds the ESP32 GPIO limit of 3.3V. The HC-SR501 outputs 3.3V natively and is read with a simple digitalRead().

Problem

A large share of startups fail from financial, operational or market problems. The goal was to find patterns linked to success or failure and build a reliable classifier.

Workflow

  • Data: numerical (funding, milestones) and categorical (industry, country) features
  • Preprocessing: missing values, categorical encoding, feature scaling, target conversion
  • EDA: distributions, correlations and feature importance
  • Models: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest and others
  • Metrics: precision, recall, F1-score and accuracy

Outcome and next steps

The best model reached about 94% precision and recall on unseen data. Work lives in a Jupyter notebook.

Planned next, per the repo: a Flask or FastAPI web app, SHAP or LIME explainability and deep-learning comparisons.

HR Analytics & Attrition

Business intelligence workflow

End-to-end analysis of the IBM HR Analytics dataset, from raw Excel data to an interactive Power BI dashboard that shows where attrition concentrates.

  • Excel cleaning and four engineered columns
  • MySQL queries for KPIs and breakdowns
  • DAX measures and filterable dashboard

Business questions

  • What is the overall attrition rate?
  • Which departments and job roles lose the most people?
  • Does overtime contribute to attrition?
  • Which groups are at higher risk of leaving?
  • What can improve retention?

Pipeline

  • Excel: cleaning, validation, pivot tables
  • Feature engineering: Attrition_Flag, Age_Group, Income_Band, Experience_Band
  • MySQL: aggregations and KPI queries
  • Power BI: DAX measures, KPI cards and filters by department, role, age group and gender

Findings

  • Research & Development lost the most people (133), then Sales (92).
  • Laboratory Technicians had the highest role-level turnover (62).
  • Overtime, junior experience and frequent travel all showed higher attrition.

Recommendations: reduce overtime, add mentorship for junior staff and focus retention on R&D.
